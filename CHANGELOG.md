# Changelog

All notable user-facing changes to this project are documented here.

## Initial Release [20260814]

- Star Trek: Strategic Operations Simulator and Tac/Scan.
- Cycle-based Z80 Sega G80 machine core running at the original 3.867120 MHz.
- Game-specific Sega 315-0064 and 315-0076 address-security implementations.
- Native G80 vector sequencer with a doubled-density shadow DDA and full
  six-bit `RRGGBB` color.
- 1080p, 720p, 720p at 120 Hz, 480p, 480i, and 240p output with four-way
  orientation and Open Matte.
- CRT profiles with bloom, halo, phosphor decay, color, and slot-mask
  controls.
- Spinner, mouse, analog-stick, and digital rotation controls with adjustable
  direction and sensitivity.
- Universal Sound Board audio with schematic-derived filtering and mixing,
  plus Star Trek speech.
- Game-specific controls, DIP switches, self-test support, and release MRAs.
